What is a marriage biodata?

A marriage biodata (also called matrimonial biodata or marriage CV) is a one-page document shared with prospective matches and their families during the marriage search process in India. It contains personal details, family background, education, career, and expectations from a life partner. It is the first impression in arranged marriage introductions.

What to include in a marriage biodata

  • Personal details: Full name, date of birth, age, height, complexion, blood group, birthplace
  • Religion & caste: Religion, caste, sub-caste, gotra, manglik status
  • Education & career: Highest qualification, occupation, employer, annual income
  • Family details: Parents' names and occupations, siblings
  • Lifestyle: Diet (vegetarian/non-veg), hobbies, smoking/drinking habits
  • Partner expectations: Preferred age range, other expectations
  • Contact details: City, phone, email (of candidate or parent)
  • Photo: A recent, clear photograph (recommended)

Tips for a good marriage biodata

  • Keep it to one page — families receive many biodatas; concise ones get read
  • Use a recent, natural-looking photograph (not too formal, not casual)
  • Be accurate and honest — family details will be verified
  • For partner expectations, be specific but not too restrictive
  • Include a parent or trusted relative's contact, not just yours
Is biodata different from a resume?

A professional resume focuses on work experience and skills. A marriage biodata focuses on personal background, family details, and lifestyle — information relevant for a life partner. The two documents have completely different purposes and audiences. Some sections overlap (education, occupation) but the overall context is different.
