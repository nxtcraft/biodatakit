How to Create a Marriage Biodata

A step-by-step guide with filled examples. Takes about 5 minutes. No signup, completely free — download as PDF when done.

Step 1
Open BiodataKit and choose a template
Go to nxtcraft.github.io/biodatakit. You'll see 3 template options at the top of the form:
  • Classic — Traditional layout with bordered header. Best for conservative/traditional families.
  • Modern — Two-column layout with a coloured header band. Looks professional and polished.
  • Simple — Clean table-based format. Works well for printing on any printer.
💡 Tip: When in doubt, choose Classic — it's the most universally accepted format across all regions and communities in India.
Step 2
Upload your photo
Click the round photo circle on the form. Select a photo from your device. Your photo is only stored in your browser tab — it is never uploaded to any server.
💡 Best photo tips: Recent photo (last 6 months) · Wear formal or semi-formal clothes · Plain background · Natural expression · No sunglasses or heavy filters
Step 3
Fill in personal details
Fill each field as it applies to you. The preview on the right updates live.

Step 7 (Optional)
Add custom fields for anything extra
Scroll to "Additional Custom Details" at the bottom of the form. Click + Add Custom Field to add any extra information.
Common custom fields people add

Own PropertyFamily owns house in Jaipur
ReferencePandit Ramesh Trivedi, Jaipur (family priest)
Step 8
Download as PDF
Click the "⬇️ Download PDF" button at the top-right of the preview panel (or at the bottom of the form). Your browser will open its print dialog.
  • For Destination: select "Save as PDF"
  • For Paper size: select A4
  • For Margins: select Default or Minimum
  • Uncheck "Headers and footers" if you don't want page URL printed
💡 The PDF saves with your name as the filename. Share it on WhatsApp, email, or print it directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data saved on BiodataKit's servers?

No. Your data never leaves your browser tab. BiodataKit has no server — it's a static website. Your photo, personal details, and family information are only in your browser's memory while the tab is open. When you close the tab, everything is cleared.

Can I edit the biodata and re-download?

Yes — just keep the tab open and update any field. The preview updates instantly. You can download as PDF as many times as you want with different details. If you accidentally close the tab, you'll need to fill in the form again (the data is not stored).

What size should the photo be?

There's no strict size limit. The tool automatically resizes your photo to fit the allocated space. A photo of at least 300×400 pixels will look sharp in the PDF. Very small photos may look blurry — use the clearest photo available.

Can I share this biodata on Shaadi.com or Jeevansathi?

This tool creates a PDF document for direct sharing (WhatsApp, email, print). Matrimonial websites require you to create an account and fill their online form — they don't accept PDF uploads directly. However, many families share the PDF biodata alongside their online profile when seriously interested.
